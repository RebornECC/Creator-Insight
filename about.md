# Creator Insight

A quick scan of your level, right from the editor pause menu.

## What you get

- **Counts.** Total objects, triggers, solid blocks, hazards, decoration and everything else.
- **Group map.** A grid of Group IDs 1 to 150. Yellow cells are used, the white one is the first free ID.
- **Free IDs.** The first free Group ID and Item ID, with one tap to copy each. No more hunting for an unused number.
- **Busiest groups.** The three groups with the most objects and triggers on them.
- **Heavy level warning.** A warning when the object count goes over the limit you set.

## How to use

1. Open your level in the editor.
2. Open the pause menu and press **Insight** in the bottom right corner.
3. Press **Rescan** after you change the level.

## Good to know

- It only reads your level. It never changes anything.
- Item IDs are taken from triggers and counters, so a number used some other way may still show as free.
- Groups used as a trigger target or center are counted as used.
